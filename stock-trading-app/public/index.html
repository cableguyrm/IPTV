    What you own

    What do these columns mean?
    • Shares: how many pieces of the company you own.
    • Avg cost: the average price you paid for each share.
    • Price now: what one share is worth right now.
    • Value: shares × price now.
    • Profit/Loss: how much you'd gain or lose if you sold everything right now. It's "unrealized" until you actually sell.

    Your orders

    "Waiting" orders haven't happened yet. You can cancel them. "Done" means the trade went through.

    🟢 Practice mode

    Pretend money ($10,000) and simulated prices. Nothing to set up. Prices move every couple of seconds so you can see how trading works.

    🔵 Paper trading keys

    Real market prices, pretend money. Free.

    1. Sign up for free at alpaca.markets and log in.
    2. In the dashboard, make sure the account switcher says Paper.
    3. Find API Keys and click Generate. Copy both the Key ID and the Secret Key (the secret is only shown once).
    4. Paste them below and click Save & test.

    🔴 Live trading keys

    REAL money. Only set this up after the Learn tab's checklist in lesson 11. Requires an approved and funded Alpaca brokerage account (availability depends on your country).

    1. In Alpaca, apply for a live account, pass the identity checks, and add money.
    2. Switch the dashboard to your Live account and generate Live API keys. These are different from paper keys.
    3. Paste them below. Set a safety limit you're comfortable with.

    🔒 Safety & privacy

    • Your keys are saved only on this computer, in keys.local.json, and are never shown to the web page.
    • The app only listens on your own computer (localhost).
    • Live mode needs you to type REAL MONEY to unlock, tick a box on every order, and respects your safety limit.
    • Never share your Secret Key with anyone. Anyone who has it can trade in your account.
    • This app is for learning. It is not financial advice.
